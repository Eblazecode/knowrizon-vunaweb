CORE OPAC

CORE is the world’s largest aggregator of open access content with millions of full texts, deriving from thousands of repositories, journals, and other web resources. It provides a wealth of research materials.

ARXVI OPAC

ARXIV is a repository of e-prints in physics, math, computer science, biology, finance, and stats. It provides open access to a wealth of research papers, allowing researchers to share their findings with the global community.

VUA REPOSITORY

Veritas University Repository is a digital archive that collects, preserves, and disseminates the intellectual output of Veritas University, including theses, dissertations, and research papers.

DOAJ OPAC

DOAJ is a community-curated online directory that indexes and provides access to high-quality, open access, peer-reviewed journals. it offers a vast collection of scholarly articles ensuring wide accessibility and visibility for researchers.
